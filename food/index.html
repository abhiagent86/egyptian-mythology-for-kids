Vegetarian Food in Egypt

Egypt is a great place to eat if you do not eat meat. Beans, lentils, rice, bread and vegetables are in many favourite dishes. Here are twelve things to try.

What the labels mean

  • No meatThe usual recipe has no meat, chicken or fish.
  • Has milkMade with milk, butter or cheese, or with nuts.
  • Ask firstSome cooks add meat or meat stock. Ask a grown-up to check.

Big dishes

  • Koshari

    say: KOH-sha-ree

    Rice, lentils, pasta and chickpeas with tangy tomato sauce and crispy onions on top. It is the most famous street food in Egypt.

    No meatNo milk

  • Ful medames

    say: FOOL meh-DAH-mis

    Soft, slow-cooked fava beans with oil, lemon and cumin. Egyptians love it for breakfast, scooped up with bread.

    No meatNo milk

  • Mahshi

    say: MAH-shee

    Vegetables such as courgettes, peppers and vine leaves, stuffed with rice, tomato and herbs.

    Often no meatAsk first

  • Molokhia

    say: mo-lo-KHEE-ya

    A bright green, garlicky soup made from chopped leaves. You eat it with rice or bread.

    Ask first

  • Lentil soup

    Egyptians call it shorbet ads

    A warm, golden soup made from red lentils, with a squeeze of lemon. Perfect for a cool evening.

    No meatOften no milk

Bread and snacks

  • Ta'ameya

    say: tah-MAY-ya

    Egyptian falafel. It is made from fava beans and herbs, so it is green inside and crispy outside.

    No meatNo milk

  • Aish baladi

    say: AYSH BAL-a-dee

    Round, puffy flatbread, baked in a very hot oven. The word "aish" also means "life" in Egypt.

    No meatNo milk

  • Feteer

    say: fe-TEER

    A flaky, buttery pastry made of many thin layers. Try it with honey or with cheese.

    No meatHas milk

Sweet treats

  • Om Ali

    say: OM AH-lee

    A warm pudding of pastry, milk, nuts and raisins, baked until golden on top.

    No meatHas milkHas nuts

  • Basbousa

    say: bas-BOO-sa

    A soft, sweet cake made from semolina and soaked in syrup, often with an almond on top.

    No meatHas milkMay have nuts

Drinks

  • Karkade

    say: kar-ka-DAY

    A deep red drink made from hibiscus flowers. You can have it hot or icy cold.

    No meatNo milk

  • Sugarcane juice

    Egyptians call it asab

    Fresh, sweet, pale green juice squeezed from sugarcane while you watch.

    No meatNo milk

Three food facts

  • Bread, beans, lentils and onions were everyday food in the time of the pharaohs too.
  • The workers who built the pyramids were paid in bread, not in coins. Money had not been invented yet.
  • Koshari has ideas from many places in it: rice and lentils from India, pasta from Italy and tomato sauce from Egypt.

For grown-ups: Recipes change from kitchen to kitchen. Molokhia is often cooked in chicken or rabbit stock, mahshi can contain minced meat, and many dishes use ghee. Always check with the cook, especially for allergies to nuts, milk, wheat or sesame.
