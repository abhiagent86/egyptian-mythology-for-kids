The Story of Egypt

Egypt is one of the oldest countries in the world. Its story is more than 5,000 years long. Here it is, from the first farmers to today.

How to read the years

BCE means "before year 1". The bigger the BCE number, the longer ago it was. CE years are the ones we count today, like 2026.

Timeline

  1. About 5000 BCE

    Farmers by the Nile

    People build villages beside the river Nile. Every year the river floods and leaves rich, dark mud that is perfect for growing food.

  2. About 3100 BCE

    One country, one king

    King Narmer joins the two lands of Egypt, the north and the south, into one country. Around this time people start to write with hieroglyphs.

  3. About 2650 BCE

    The first pyramid

    King Djoser's Step Pyramid is built at Saqqara. It is the first big building in the world made of cut stone.

  4. About 2560 BCE

    The Great Pyramid

    The Great Pyramid is built at Giza for King Khufu. For almost 4,000 years it is the tallest building in the world.

  5. About 2055 BCE

    The Middle Kingdom

    After a time of trouble, Egypt is joined together again. People write famous stories and trade with far-away lands.

  6. About 1550 BCE

    The New Kingdom begins

    Egypt becomes richer and stronger than ever. Kings are now called pharaohs and are buried in hidden tombs in the Valley of the Kings.

  7. About 1470 BCE

    Queen Hatshepsut

    A woman called Hatshepsut rules as pharaoh. She builds a beautiful temple and sends ships to bring back trees, gold and animals.

  8. About 1332 BCE

    The boy king

    Tutankhamun becomes pharaoh when he is about nine years old. He rules for about ten years.

  9. 1279 BCE

    Ramses the Great

    Ramses II becomes pharaoh and rules for 66 years. He builds giant statues and temples all over Egypt.

  10. 332 BCE

    Alexander the Great

    A Greek king called Alexander takes over Egypt. He starts a new city by the sea called Alexandria. Greek kings rule Egypt for 300 years.

  11. 30 BCE

    Cleopatra, the last pharaoh

    Queen Cleopatra dies and Egypt becomes part of the Roman Empire. The time of the pharaohs is over.

  12. 641 CE

    Arab Egypt

    Arab armies arrive. Over time most people in Egypt begin to speak Arabic and follow the religion of Islam.

  13. 969 CE

    Cairo is born

    The city of Cairo is founded. Today it is one of the biggest cities in Africa.

  14. 1799 and 1822

    The Rosetta Stone

    French soldiers find a stone with the same message in three kinds of writing. In 1822 a Frenchman called Champollion uses it to read hieroglyphs for the first time in 1,400 years.

  15. 1869

    The Suez Canal

    A canal opens that lets ships sail between Europe and Asia without going all the way around Africa.

  16. 1922

    Tutankhamun's tomb is found

    Howard Carter finds the tomb of Tutankhamun, still full of golden treasure. In the same year Egypt becomes an independent country again.

  17. 2025

    The Grand Egyptian Museum

    A giant new museum opens next to the pyramids. All of Tutankhamun's treasures are shown together for the first time.

Three wow facts

  • Cleopatra lived closer in time to us than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
  • The time of the pharaohs lasted about 3,000 years. That is about 100 grandparents in a row.
  • When Tutankhamun was born, the pyramids were already more than 1,200 years old.

For grown-ups: Dates before about 700 BCE are estimates, and different books give slightly different years. "BCE" and "CE" are the same years as "BC" and "AD".
