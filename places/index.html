Top 10 Places in Cairo, Giza and Alexandria

Cairo is the capital city of Egypt. Giza is right next door, on the other side of the river Nile. Here are nine amazing places to see there, and one more by the sea in Alexandria.

1

The Great Pyramid of Giza

Giza

The biggest pyramid in Egypt. It was built for King Khufu about 4,500 years ago, from more than two million stone blocks. Two more big pyramids stand next to it.

Wow! It is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World that you can still visit.

2

The Great Sphinx

Giza

A giant statue with the body of a lion and the head of a king. It was carved out of one huge rock and guards the pyramids.

Wow! It is about 73 metres long. That is longer than six buses in a row.

3

The Grand Egyptian Museum

Giza

A huge new museum next to the pyramids. All the treasures from King Tutankhamun's tomb are here, with his golden mask and his throne. It opened fully in November 2025.

Wow! A statue of King Ramses II, as tall as a three-storey house, greets you in the entrance hall.

4

The Museum of Egyptian Civilization

Old Cairo

This museum tells the whole story of Egypt. In a quiet, dark hall you can see the real mummies of kings and queens, such as Ramses II and Queen Hatshepsut.

Wow! In 2021 the royal mummies were driven here across Cairo in a big golden parade.

5

The Citadel of Cairo

Islamic Cairo

A great castle on a hill, begun by the famous leader Saladin more than 800 years ago. Inside is a beautiful mosque with tall, thin towers.

Wow! From the walls you can see all of Cairo. On a clear day you can spot the pyramids.

6

The Mosque of Ibn Tulun

Old Cairo

The oldest mosque in Cairo that still looks the way it did when it was new. It was built more than 1,100 years ago, with a huge open courtyard where people come to pray.

Wow! Its tower has a staircase that winds around the outside like a snail shell. You can climb it to see over the rooftops.

7

The Hanging Church

Coptic Cairo

One of the oldest churches in Egypt. It was built on top of the gate of an old Roman fort, so it seems to hang in the air. You climb 29 steps to reach the door.

Wow! Look up inside: the wooden roof is shaped like the bottom of a boat, to remind people of Noah's Ark.

8

Khan el-Khalili Market

Islamic Cairo

A busy old market with narrow lanes. Shops sell shiny lamps, spices, sweets and toys. People have been shopping here since the 1300s.

Wow! Listen for the tap-tap-tap of craftsmen hammering patterns into metal trays.

9

A Boat Ride on the Nile

Cairo

The Nile is the longest river in Africa and it flows right through the middle of Cairo. You can ride on a sailing boat called a felucca and watch the city go by.

Wow! Almost everyone in Egypt lives close to the Nile, just as they did in the time of the pharaohs.

10

The Library of Alexandria

Alexandria, by the sea

Alexandria is a city on the sea, about three hours north of Cairo. Long ago it had the most famous library in the world. Today a giant new library stands near the same spot. Its round roof tilts towards the sea like a rising sun.

Wow! The outside wall is carved with letters from about 120 different kinds of writing. Inside there is room for eight million books.

For grown-ups: Checked in November 2026. Opening times, tickets and what is on show can change, so look at each place's own website before you go.
