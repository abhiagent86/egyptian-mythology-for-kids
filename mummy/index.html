How the levels work

  • Easy: six steps, with hints. Mistakes do not cost any days.
  • Medium: eight steps and three trick answers. Each mistake costs one day.
  • Hard: no hints, and each mistake costs three days. Finish by day 70 to win three stars.
Stuck? Show the steps in order
  1. Wash the body. The priests wash the body with water from the river Nile and sweet-smelling palm wine.
  2. Take out the insides. The lungs, liver, stomach and intestines each go into their own jar. The heart stays in, because Egyptians believed you think with your heart. And the brain? It was pulled out through the nose with a hook!
  3. Cover with salt. The body lies under a salt called natron for 40 days. The salt soaks up all the water, so the body dries out completely.
  4. Rub with oils. Oils make the dry skin soft again. Sweet-smelling tree sap helps keep insects away.
  5. Wrap in linen. The priests wrap the head first and work down to the feet. They use hundreds of metres of linen and tuck lucky charms between the layers.
  6. Add a mask and a coffin. The mask helps the spirit find its own body again. Then the mummy is laid in a painted coffin.
  7. Open the mouth. A priest touches the mummy's mouth with a special tool and says magic words. Now the king can eat, speak and breathe in the next life.
  8. Seal the tomb. The mummy is carried to its tomb with food, furniture and treasure. Then the door is closed and sealed.

Four mummy facts

  • Making a mummy took about 70 days from start to finish.
  • Anubis, the god with a jackal head, looked after mummies. The chief priest sometimes wore an Anubis mask.
  • The four jars had lids shaped like the four sons of Horus: a person, a baboon, a jackal and a falcon.
  • You can see the real mummies of kings and queens in the Museum of Egyptian Civilization in Cairo.

For grown-ups: This is a simplified version of the most careful, expensive method, as used for royal burials. Cheaper methods skipped steps, and practice changed over 3,000 years. The day counts are rounded so the total comes to about 70. The game mentions that the brain was removed through the nose; nothing is shown.
